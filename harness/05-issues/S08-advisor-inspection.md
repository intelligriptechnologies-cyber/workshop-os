# S08 — Advisor inspection and ownership

Status: Approved  
Implements: R-030, R-031

## Outcome
Give advisors accountable queues, structured inspection/evidence, follow-ups, reassignment, and promised-delivery management.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every Job has exactly one accountable advisor and audited reassignment updates the appropriate action queues.
- [ ] Required inspection findings/evidence and promised delivery are validated; follow-ups surface by due state and ownership.
