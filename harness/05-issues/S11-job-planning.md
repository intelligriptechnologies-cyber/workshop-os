# S11 — Job planning and shared timeline

Status: Approved  
Implements: R-041, R-042, R-045

## Outcome
Plan snapshotted approved scope into dependency/material/checklist-aware multi-technician tasks and a permission-filtered timeline.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Planning represents skills, bays, effort, dependencies, materials, checklists, priority, and delivery risk from the approved snapshot.
- [ ] Multi-technician responsibility/reassignment is concurrency-safe and capacity-aware.
- [ ] Each role sees the same chronology filtered to permitted evidence and actions.
