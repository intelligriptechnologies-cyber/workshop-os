# S27 — Accessibility, devices, documents, scanning, and training

Status: Approved  
Implements: R-046, R-049, R-091, R-092, R-115, R-116, R-117

## Outcome
Prove WCAG, device/browser, camera/scanner/manual fallback, rendered documents, and role usability on representative hardware/users.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Automated/manual review meets WCAG 2.2 AA across keyboard, focus, contrast, labels/errors, target size, reflow, and assistive technology.
- [ ] Critical flows, documents, QR authorization, camera/hardware scans, and manual fallback work on current Android Chrome PWA, desktop Chrome/Edge, and functionally on current iOS Safari.
- [ ] After at most two hours training, at least 90% of representative staff complete critical role scenarios unaided with zero critical-control errors.
