# S12 — Technician PWA, evidence, scanning, and synchronization

Status: Approved  
Implements: R-043, R-044, R-046, R-047, R-048, R-049

## Outcome
Ship My Tasks on Android with progression, required evidence, scanner/manual fallback, and conflict-safe drafts.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Technicians start/pause/resume/block/handoff/complete with reason/history; required evidence/checklists block completion.
- [ ] Drafts survive interruption and expose recoverable conflicts, while offline authoritative operations remain visibly blocked.
- [ ] Camera/hardware scanning validates tenant, object, state, and permission; manual fallback is reasoned/audited and critical controls are usable with large touch targets.
