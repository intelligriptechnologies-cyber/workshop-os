# S22 — Guided boards, reports, and protected exports

Status: Approved  
Implements: R-097, R-098, R-099

## Outcome
Provide role-guided operations plus defined reports, drill-through, and protected asynchronous exports.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Boards show only permitted live queues/capacity/delay/blocker/owner/next-action data in plain language.
- [ ] Defined operational, inventory, profitability, finance, customer, staff, QC/rework, and audit metrics reconcile to source ledgers and drill through safely.
- [ ] Exports remain tenant/branch filtered, audited, manifest/checksum protected, expiring, and inaccessible across tenants.
