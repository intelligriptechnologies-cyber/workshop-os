## Question

What rows, states, filters, call-log affordances, and actions should the
Reception "Expected Today" list expose before a booked customer arrives and
when they need follow-up, rescheduling, cancellation, or manual no-show?

## Resolution

Reception now has an operational-date "Expected Today" panel. It lists only
Booked, Confirmed, and Rescheduled Bookings still due on that date, with
filters for all expected customers, those needing follow-up, and those with a
call already logged. Each row shows the latest timestamped call note and gives
Reception audited actions to log a call, reschedule, cancel, or manually mark
a no-show. Terminal and moved bookings leave the list automatically.
